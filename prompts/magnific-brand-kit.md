# Prompts para Magnific — Kit de marca "Beauty Organic by Tania"

> Recomendación: usa los prompts en **inglés** (los generadores de imagen responden mejor) y sube como **imagen de referencia** el logo (`docs/referencias/logo-organic-beauty-foto-2.webp`) y la foto del producto (`docs/referencias/crema-vistas.webp`).

## 1. Prompt maestro (lámina completa del kit de marca)

```
Luxury skincare brand identity board for "Beauty Organic by Tania", a Mexican organic face and body cream for dark spots, founded by Tania in Puerto Vallarta. Elegant brand guidelines presentation sheet on a warm off-white background, editorial layout with generous negative space, Clinique and Aesop-level minimalism.

Include: primary logo with handwritten script "Beauty" in deep cocoa brown, small uppercase serif "ORGANIC" above, "by Tania" in delicate script below, two small fresh green leaves and a soft blush watercolor blob behind the wordmark; secondary horizontal logo; circular monogram "B" with a leaf; color palette swatches row (warm white #FFFFFF, sand #F8F2EC, nude blush #EBD0C4, soft terracotta #C9917F, mocha mousse #A47864, cocoa #33221E, sunset gold #D9965A, leaf green #8FAE5A); typography specimen with elegant high-contrast serif headlines and clean geometric sans-serif body text; white dome-lid cream jar 8 cm wide with the round label; stationery mockups (business card, thank-you card, kraft gift box with tissue paper); Instagram post and story templates; subtle botanical leaf pattern; sunset beach lifestyle photo thumbnail with golden light.

Soft natural daylight, premium, feminine, clean girl aesthetic, quiet luxury, warm nude tones, high-end beauty brand, ultra detailed, 4k, flat lay grid composition.
```

**Negativo / evitar:**
```
cluttered, saturated neon colors, cheap, cartoon, distorted text, misspelled words, extra letters, watermark, harsh shadows, plastic look, dark background, busy patterns
```

**Formato sugerido:** 16:9 (lámina horizontal) o 4:5 (para Instagram).
